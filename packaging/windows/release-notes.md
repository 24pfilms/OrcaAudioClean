**Download `OrcaAudioCleanup-Setup.exe` below and double-click it.**

Windows will warn you the first time, because this free app isn't signed with a paid Microsoft certificate.
Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Step-by-step help, including what your browser may ask:
https://github.com/24pfilms/OrcaAudioClean#install

Setup then downloads the engine and the voice models (about 2 GB, once, 10 to 20 minutes).

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Mac version: https://github.com/24pfilms/OrcaAudioCleanup-Mac/releases/latest
